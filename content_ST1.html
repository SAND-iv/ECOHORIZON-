Global Warming: The Rising Threat

Global warming is the steady rise in the Earth's average temperature, caused mainly by increased carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases in the atmosphere. This warming is leading to drastic climate changes worldwide including stronger storms, rising sea levels, and melting glaciers.A major cause of global warming is the burning of fossil fuels like coal, oil, and gas, which releases heat-trapping gases into the atmosphere. The impact is visible in longer heatwaves, wildfires, and loss of biodiversity.

What Causes Global Warming?

🔥

Fossil Fuels

Burning coal, oil and gas releases CO₂, trapping heat in the atmosphere

🌲

Deforestation

Fewer trees mean less CO₂ absorption, worsening the greenhouse effect

🏭

Industry

Factories emit harmful gases, accelerating climate change

🐄

Agriculture

Livestock produce methane, a potent greenhouse gas

💡

Energy Waste

Excessive electricity use increases carbon footprints

Effects of Global Warming

Melting Ice Caps

Melting glaciers and polar ice are raising sea levels and threatening Arctic wildlife.

Rising Sea Levels

Coastal cities face flooding risks, forcing relocation and damaging ecosystems.

Extreme Weather Events

Heatwaves, hurricanes, and wildfires are becoming more intense and frequent.

Damage to Coral Reefs

Warmer oceans are causing coral reefs to turn white and die, a process called coral bleaching. This destroys habitats for many sea animals and affects fishing and tourism.

Water Shortages

Hotter temperatures cause rivers, lakes, and reservoirs to dry up. Many places are now facing droughts and don't have enough clean water for drinking, farming, or everyday use.

How You Can Help

🚲

Use Sustainable Transport

Choose walking, cycling or public transport instead of driving

💡

Switch to Renewables

Use solar/wind energy and energy-efficient appliances

🥗

Eat Plant-Based

Reduce meat consumption and choose local, seasonal foods

👕

Avoid Fast Fashion

Buy quality clothing that lasts and support eco-friendly brands

📢

Spread Awareness

Educate others and support climate-friendly policies

Your choices matter — even small changes help fight global warming.

Challenges in Fighting Global Warming

Dependence on Fossil Fuels

Many countries still rely on coal, oil, and gas for energy, making it harder to switch to cleaner alternatives.

Misinformation

False information about climate change slows public support for solutions

Financial Costs

Transitioning to green technologies requires upfront investment, which some countries and businesses struggle to afford
